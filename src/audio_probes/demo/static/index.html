Audio Probe Lab

Build a sound, play it, and see what each of Whisper's 13 encoder layers "knows" about it. Each layer has a tiny trained probe (a weighted sum) that reads one fact out of that layer. Change a slider and the clip is re-made and re-measured in about a second.
